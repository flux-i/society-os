## Outcome and authority

A current resident can submit a private move-in, move-out or contact-review checklist for their own currently entitled home. A registry administrator can prepare one for a supplied current person/home. The responsible administrator records the checks and a different eligible administrator makes the final completion decision. Keep a useful outstanding queue and original history beside Homes & people; preserve the forest/ivory interface and accessible shared controls.

Only current registry administration grants the private operational desk, preparation on another person's behalf and staff checking/completion. Treasury, audit and committee read access alone do not grant those changes. Resident submissions are restricted to the actor's current linked identity/home, with no other household's people or contact selector. Own submissions retain a limited personal history after a move; ending membership removes fresh household details, recipient links and new submissions. Recheck current authority inside reserved writes and before accepted-operation replay. Privileged decisions require fresh verification and MFA.

Completion records a deliberate review. It never changes occupancy or membership, grants an account, verifies contact consent, uploads evidence, posts a charge, issues a receipt or dispatches a message automatically. Operators use the existing permitted Registry, Contacts, Documents and Access workflows for those changes. Missing supplied evidence remains incomplete; the checklist cannot invent confirmation from a checkbox alone.

## Retained proposal and checks

A checklist retains its kind, exact home/person identity, supplied effective date, explanation and immutable proposal version. Use bounded source/reference notes rather than copied phone numbers, identity documents or bank details. Allow a pending proposal to be revised by its author and deliberately withdrawn. Preserve a declined or completed record and every decision; a later correction is a linked separately reviewed version, never deletion of the original.

Show a fixed, small checklist appropriate to the kind. Identity/source verification, registry relationship/occupancy review, registered contact/preferences review, relevant document/access handover and practical handover are explicit checks. Each check has incomplete, checked or deliberately not-applicable state, a reason/source reference, actor and time. Not-applicable requires an explanation and remains visible to the final reviewer. No assumed fees, liability or statutory documentation list.

Registry and contact tasks open the existing permitted record. Retain the reviewed registry/contact version in staff evidence and recheck it before completion. A changed source invalidates the prior ready-for-review confirmation, preserves the human notes and asks for a current review. A contact profile's verified state remains distinct from this administrative check; no checklist action grants opt-in. Move-out keeps a named source-date record rather than assuming that a requested date ends the current membership.

Require a deliberate final preview listing the exact person/home, kind/date, all checks and remaining source warnings. Completion is unavailable with a missing required check, stale source or pending evidence. The final reviewer must differ from the author and the administrator who submitted the checked proposal. Separate decline/request-information and own withdrawal preserve the record. A correction of a completed checklist leaves the accepted predecessor effective until separately accepted.

## Queue, privacy and retries

Use bounded pages with independent full counts for awaiting checks, awaiting separate review, information requested, completed and closed-without-completion. Search is limited to current permitted records. No public list of movers, personal contact data or unverified allegations. Personal history contains only the actor's submissions and permitted retained outcome; current household information is resolved separately.

Every write uses a current version, explicit confirmation and actor-bound stable operation key. Identical accepted retries return the original durable result. Different payloads or competing current versions conflict without duplicate checks/decisions. An unknown response freezes editing/dismissal and retries that same request. A stale reload preserves supplied human text where applicable and requires a renewed preview and confirmation. Pending actions, source errors and expired access remain explicit; unavailable checks are never shown as completed.

## Implementation decisions before code

Use three additive STRICT resource/immutable-version/event tables, bringing 93 persistent tables to 96. Preserve every old definition, row, SQL object and migration checksum. Every check, ready proposal, decision and correction receives an immutable version and exact event; head changes require the matching event. Approved predecessors remain readable while a correction is checked or reviewed. Home/person/kind are fixed identities.

Identity, registry and contact checks are required; documents/access and practical handover permit explicit not-applicable reasons. Server-recorded actors/times and a current flat/membership/contact fingerprint bind each check. CHECKING → READY requires all applicable evidence to match the current source; completion requires an exact current preview and a different fresh registry administrator from original author, current proposal author and checking submitter. Source changes preserve notes but prevent completion until rechecked.

An explicit current response flag permits registry administrators and a linked resident identity to read this workspace. Residents see only their own submissions; current membership permits new household submissions/revisions, while limited own outcome history and own pending withdrawal remain after moving out. Former administrator authors do not retain other households’ access. Wider committee/finance grants do not confer staff checking.
