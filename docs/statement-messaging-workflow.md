## Outcome and authority

A currently eligible Treasury operator selects a specific currently published financial statement in Messages, chooses a channel and bounded target, previews its exact current audience/verified finance-consent intersection, and proposes delivery. A different currently eligible Treasury reviewer approves that frozen source, content and audience. Authenticated portal links identify the exact original. Finance preparation/publication and messaging decisions remain separate. Registry or community review alone cannot prepare or approve a statement message. Current authority and privileged verification apply before writes and successful replay.

The source is a distinct `STATEMENT` with purpose `FINANCE`, bound to the publication identity, original file/checksum and frozen publication target. The external envelope says a published financial statement is available and contains the authenticated link; it omits figures, original bytes and private preparation/review history. Owners and tenants receive only deliberately published current access. A tenant needs current publication access and verified finance-channel consent, not private home-ledger permission. Delivery creates no payment, charge, allocation or receipt.

Preview distinguishes selected target people, source-entitled people, consented people, eligible linked accounts, unique destinations and omissions. Current publication/version, both approving operators, membership/account and consent are checked again at approval, claim and handoff. Revoked or superseded publications suppress new handoffs; the queue never substitutes a replacement. Unknown handoffs reconcile retained proof, while prior accepted/delivered/read outcomes remain historical facts. A copied file or provider-accepted message cannot be recalled. Current channel delivery uses the existing persistent local adapter; live onboarding remains a separate input.

Resident message history redacts private target IDs, actors, recipients and preparation information. If the original publication is no longer accessible, the source link is removed while truthful personal delivery history remains. Read-only native WebMCP reveals bounded source/status metadata, preserves human forms and discards cancelled or stale-scope results; it performs no decisions or dispatch.
