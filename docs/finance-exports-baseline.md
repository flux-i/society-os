## Outcome and scope

Entries, Receipts, Maintenance and Collections each open the shared export dialog with the relevant report selected. A person chooses source dates and a permitted scope, reviews current totals and retained links, confirms one immutable snapshot, and separately downloads its verified original bytes. Saved snapshots reopen while the actor retains their exact original authority and every frozen home. A new export captures later changes. Source-date filtering is distinct from current home balance and current corrections; these operational reports do not create a statutory historical close.

Society and Treasury home scopes require current Treasury authority and fresh privileged verification. All-own-home exports resolve actual financially entitled memberships even when the actor also has broader community/audit authority. Tenant society-statement access does not allow household finance export. Current access is checked before preview, creation, replay, listing, metadata and download. An ended frozen home denies the entire old personal snapshot; gaining another home does not expand an accepted copy.

Original receipts and linked entry, allocation, contribution and exemption corrections remain identifiable. Integer paise stay exact decimal strings through JSON, UI and CSV. Opening credit, cash received, original receipt totals, purpose outstanding and current net balance remain separate. Unconfirmed reports contribute no money or receipt; private report evidence/comments and staff explanations stay outside exports. Formula-leading untrusted text receives a visible text marker without changing its stored original. Initial limits are 10,000 output rows, 5 MiB per snapshot, 100 MiB per actor and 1 GiB society-wide stored exports; overflow creates no snapshot/audit side effect.
