## Behavior and independent expectations

Work follows Planned → In progress → Waiting / Ready for check → Done. The actor submitting for checking cannot confirm their own completion; a different current operator can complete or return it with a reason. Cancellation/reopening retain immutable events. An expired operational assignment remains in history and counts as needing reassignment; it cannot be chosen for new work. Inactive vendors/retired assets stay readable in historical links but are excluded from new choices. Register choices are bounded to 500 active records per kind.

Due, visit, AMC and inspection dates are explicitly supplied India calendar dates. Tomorrow is not overdue; yesterday counts once while open. Done/cancelled work drops from operator open obligations. Recurrence previews and confirms a new dated linked occurrence; it never edits the source, renews an AMC or advances an inspection automatically. Concurrent transitions have one winner; exact response-loss retries preserve the resulting identity/event and check current authority before replay.

Resident publication freezes its supplied title/body plus current work category, priority, status and dates. Private changes leave its public version, counts, search and timestamps unchanged. Explicit republishing refreshes it; removal immediately denies current resident reads.
