# Private reminders and delivery exceptions — before-code expectations

Recorded 8 October 2026 while meeting/acknowledgement acceptance finishes. Implementation follows its accepted checkpoint. This extends the existing maintenance, collections, meeting and reviewed messaging workflows in the [operations roadmap](society-operations-roadmap.md).

## Intended outcome

An authorised operator can identify current outstanding supplied maintenance/fund amounts or deliberately requested meeting acknowledgements, prepare a private reminder for an explicitly reviewed selection, and follow its delivery exceptions. Residents see only reminders and delivery status addressed to their own current permitted identity. Preserve the approved forest/ivory interface, accessible shared controls and bounded attention queues.

This workflow uses confirmed supplied records. Unverified payment reports do not count as receipts. A reminder creates no charge, interest, fine, payment initiation, attendance, vote or consent. A passed supplied deadline is a selection criterion, with its exact meaning shown. It does not establish statutory default or authorise a sanction.

## Source authority and private eligibility

Maintenance/fund reminder preparation, review and dispatch require current separate finance authority, enrolled factors and fresh confirmation. Meeting acknowledgement reminders require current community authority and the exact currently published requested version. A different eligible actor reviews the exact frozen proposal before any deliberate handoff. Registry administration alone does not confer finance access.

Freeze the source identity/version/fingerprint and explicit criteria, supplied deadline/date basis, content, purpose, selected people and reviewed contact preferences. Preview full eligible/omitted counts independently of pagination, with a bounded selected-recipient page. Keep a person with several relevant homes distinct from several people sharing one destination. Never silently add newly eligible people or expand a frozen home area.

For a meeting, select distinct current activated members in the approved frozen homes whose personal acknowledgement of that exact publication remains outstanding. Pending private replacements keep the approved predecessor eligible. A published successor, cancellation, withdrawal, ended membership, disabled account or completed acknowledgement removes eligibility for a new handoff of the former reminder.

For maintenance/funds, derive exact paise from published supplied charges, linked corrections and confirmed current allocations. Preserve each home's credit and purpose boundaries. Recheck the relevant current financial membership and deliberately verified finance contact preference. An exempt/corrected/fully allocated obligation or withdrawn/closed source must not receive a new outstanding-amount reminder. Do not allocate unrelated held credits or infer settlement from a pending report.

Phone/email destinations, household balances, acknowledgement identities and non-responder lists remain private to authorised operational views. The resident and native views cannot expose other people's reminder status. Use authenticated current source links; the existence of an old delivery does not restore access to a withdrawn record or former home.

## Review, handoff and exceptions

Reuse the existing immutable messaging proposal, different-person approval, actor-bound operation identity, explicit dispatch and per-attempt outcome model. Add the necessary exact reminder source binding rather than creating an automatic sender. Preserve prior notice, receipt, statement and uncertain-provider originals during any schema extension.

An acknowledged meeting or paid/corrected obligation becomes an explicit retained omission; it must not be silently sent or transformed into an unrelated successor reminder.

An uncertain attempt requires reconciliation of that same attempt. Reconciliation retains its original evidence even if current eligibility has ended and must not create a new delivery. A definite failure can receive an explicitly reviewed, bounded retry only after fresh eligibility checks. Accepted-operation retries return the original durable action without duplication; changed payloads conflict. Keep queued, accepted, delivered, explicit read, skipped, definite failure and uncertain states distinct.

The private exception view groups current actionable outcomes and offers exact links to existing review/reconciliation controls. Derive full counts independently of visible rows. Opening a queue, publishing a meeting, recording an acknowledgement or confirming money never dispatches a reminder. Keep real-provider activation and its supplied configuration separate from the local simulated workflow.

## Implementation decisions before code

Inspection of the existing schema shows that `message_batches` has a constrained source-kind/purpose CHECK. Extend that one parent using the existing dedicated-connection create/copy/drop/rename procedure, restore foreign-key enforcement on every exit and check all retained references before commit. Preserve all 85 prior tables' rows, the other 84 table definitions, existing indexes/triggers and original migration checksums. Add one immutable per-person reminder binding table. Historical schema-20-to-21 acceptance continues to inspect its genuine boundary before migrating to the current application.

Use three explicit source kinds: maintenance reminder, fixed fund reminder and meeting acknowledgement reminder. Operators choose outstanding records or a passed supplied deadline. A missing deadline never qualifies as passed; voluntary campaigns never become debts. The frozen meeting source is its exact approved publication, including its audience and acknowledgement request; a pending successor leaves it available. Financial sources retain supplied participant homes and dates, while each eligible person's binding records exact active charges/allocations and remaining paise in their current permitted participating homes. Several relevant homes count once per person. Unrelated credits and pending payment reports do not reduce these amounts.

The preview hash includes the criterion, source, exact selected people, their per-person bindings, contacts, content and omissions. Changed outstanding amounts require a fresh proposal/review; an already approved reminder skips that person before a new handoff. Completed acknowledgements or settled/exempt/corrected obligations become explicit retained omissions. Shared destinations may carry one generic authenticated portal link, with no household amounts, participant names, meeting wording or non-responder lists in the external envelope. The existing separate approval covers at most three deliberate attempts; each definite-failure retry still requires the visible operator confirmation and fresh eligibility. UNKNOWN reconciliation addresses its original attempt even after source eligibility ends.

Delivery exceptions remain a private operator view with independent full counts, bounded pages and exact destination-page links into the existing approved-message controls. Residents retain only their own approved delivery history with current source access; reminder bindings and other people's amounts/status remain private. Native tools expose only bounded statuses and discard held reminder/exception snapshots after current source/eligibility or authority changes. No automatic scheduling or dispatch is introduced.

A pending reminder’s recipient refresh may update current amounts and contacts within its frozen source. It cannot replace that source publication or expand its frozen homes. A changed/withdrawn meeting publication disables recipient refresh while retaining own withdrawal and different-person decline. The operator prepares a new reminder and reviews the new source explicitly.
