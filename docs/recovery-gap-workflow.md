# Financial recovery-gap review — before-code contract

Prepared during the frozen 0.34 regression run. Implement only after the [independent receipt-numbering checkpoint](recovery-numbering-workflow.md) is accepted, privately archived and personally published. The [parent recovery contract](restore-reconciliation-workflow.md) remains incomplete. Standing authorization to continue complete verified workflows covers this fictional development; real finance, custody and society policy remains a production input.

A current recovery reviewer needs to compare the restored financial record with separately supplied records covering the gap after the checkpoint. A fresh receipt prefix prevents reuse of numbers; it does not reconstruct missing transactions or prove that all money and receipts survived. This workflow records and separately reviews those differences before the later cutover can consume them.

The operator records the known coverage interval, independently supplied source/book references, exact home and money differences, receipt/source identities where applicable, and explicit unresolved evidence. The portal previews the effect on the checkpoint balance. Another current freshly authenticated person approves or declines the frozen version, keeping the original submission, independent decision and linked replacement. Review is preparation: it neither posts entries nor issues receipts, grants ordinary access, sends messages or releases the recovered host.

For example, the existing source fixture retains its 40000-paise receipt and linked reversal, with a checkpoint balance of 150001 paise. A separately supplied, already-received 25001-paise item gives an independently expected comparison balance of 125000 paise. The original remains unchanged. The journal must distinguish a reviewed known difference from missing or contradictory evidence; a matching total alone cannot establish complete recovery.

## Existing code and boundaries

`internal/database/recovery_review.go` and `recovery_numbering.go` already enforce the current ordered epoch/appointment, real MFA, fresh writes, separate supplied person references, exact version/input/actor retries, bounded pages and retained linked history. Reuse those permission and transaction patterns. Normal entries, receipt/PDF snapshots, corrections and public numbers are held by the existing recovery checks and SQL triggers.

`internal/database/identity.go` currently withholds all checkpoint roles and memberships during recovery. Removing quarantine alone would expose its ordinary historical-grant queries again. Final cutover must establish reviewed current authority explicitly; this financial journal cannot substitute for it. Likewise, `receipt_issue.go` uses the original yearly counter and must remain held until the later transaction selects the independently reserved new series. Neither a gap approval nor a source receipt reference reopens that counter.

Scheduled captures and external jobs also remain held. An archive may contain a capture in progress, or a message whose reply was lost. Financial reconciliation cannot turn those old identities into permission to capture new bytes or resend. Their retained dispositions and current separate decisions remain independent cutover prerequisites.

The existing amount parser is `internal/database/records.go:ParseAmount`: positive rupee decimal strings become exact integer paise, with the existing 1000000000-paise per-item cap. Existing account balances include confirmed debits minus confirmed credits plus live voluntary-fund contributions, excluding linked reversals. The gap comparison must use those same retained accounting facts; treating every received fund contribution as a maintenance credit would change the meaning of the balance. A recovered reviewer has no ordinary finance permission, so the bounded recovery read needs its own current-reviewer guard rather than reopening the ordinary records endpoint.

## Planned retained journal

Append checksummed migration 32, preserving every preceding row, BLOB, SQL object and provenance entry. Store immutable frozen gap preparations and separate decisions with current source, appointment, author/person binding, operation identity, supplied coverage/evidence, exact manual amounts and linked predecessor/version. Use a small bounded item inventory; bound notes, references, requests, pages and history rather than admitting arbitrary SQL, archives or unbounded record lists.

Money uses the existing exact amount parser and integer paise. Known items distinguish supplied given debits/charges, already-received money and evidence concerning an existing record or receipt. The journal records each item once within its supplied book/source namespace; a reference or checksum is an attested input, not automatic bank verification or a downloaded original. Preserve supplied legacy printed numbers and original identity references without allocating a new number during review. Missing, contradictory or unverifiable items stay visible and cannot be relabelled complete to make the totals agree.

For the first bounded inventory, distinguish an account debit, account money already received and evidence only. Financial differences name an exact existing home. An evidence-only item can describe a supplied fund allocation, legacy receipt or uncertainty without pretending its accounting effect has been reconstructed. A reference to a confirmed checkpoint entry must match its home, identity and amount and contributes no additional debit or credit. A missing fund allocation or contradictory reference stays unresolved for final disposition. Bind each supplied book/item identity to one current preparation, permit an explicit linked replacement, and reject duplicate inclusion across unrelated preparations for the same recovery source. Limit each preparation to 24 items and bound aggregate pages/history; an empty declared gap needs an explicit whole-coverage attestation rather than an inferred zero.

Read the checkpoint balance from existing confirmed entries and linked corrections. Compute the supplied comparison with checked integer arithmetic and show checkpoint amount, proposed difference and comparison amount separately. Reject ambiguous decimals, overflow, another home, duplicate item identity, inconsistent source references and a changed retry body. A declared empty gap requires explicit independently supplied coverage/evidence and a separate decision; absence of submitted rows does not prove that nothing was lost.

Independent expectations must include an existing voluntary contribution and a reversed contribution, alongside the simple 150001 minus 25001 comparison. A matching existing-entry reference changes the comparison by zero. Preserve failure on malformed or overflowing input rather than rounding, clamping or letting a sum error become a zero balance. The preparation records its own checkpoint/comparison amounts; it does not relabel those figures as posted resident dues.

Only different current freshly authenticated reviewers can decide a frozen version. Self-review, stale source/appointment/version, copied or expired/revoked authority, missing evidence and conflicting decisions fail without financial effects. Exact lost-reply retries resolve the one actor-bound retained result. A changed source or linked replacement invalidates older preparation for current cutover while retaining its historical contents and decision.

The later final cutover must separately decide how accepted known differences become confirmed records while preserving any independently available original receipt/PDF and identity. This checkpoint does not claim reconstruction, zero data loss, ordinary authority or release. In particular, a 125000-paise comparison is not represented as the stored balance while the journal is still preparation.
