# WhatsApp sending inside the portal — before-code expectations

Recorded before implementation, 7 October 2026. This follows [scoped finance exports](finance-exports-workflow.md), whose full acceptance is pending when this contract is saved. The user rejects a workflow that requires opening WhatsApp Messenger and pressing Send. This requirement supersedes the unimplemented manual receipt/statement sharing proposal. Existing local messaging remains a simulation until an official provider is separately configured and activated.

## User outcome and external boundary

An authorised operator prepares a notice, original receipt or exact published statement, selects the permitted registered recipients, and reviews the content and recipient count inside Society OS. A different eligible reviewer approves it. The operator clicks Send in the portal; a configured backend adapter submits the approved messages to the official WhatsApp Business Platform. No staff WhatsApp Messenger action is required. The portal shows queued, provider accepted, delivered, explicitly reported read, failed, skipped and uncertain outcomes separately.

Reuse the existing [targeting, independent approval, consent and durable dispatch contract](messaging-workflow.md), and [exact statement publication and audience intersection](statement-messaging-workflow.md). Preserve the existing synthetic adapter and its explicit labels for local development. An unconfigured or disabled live provider cannot send, claim a real success or fall back silently to simulation. Implement and exercise the provider boundary with local HTTP fixtures; no real external message or paid account activation is authorised by the feature/pricing questions.

## Pricing and category meaning

Meta's developer documentation and its downloadable INR rate card were read directly on 7 October 2026. The card is effective 1 October 2026 and lists Indian recipient-market rates of ₹0.115 per delivered utility, domestic authentication and charged service message; marketing is ₹0.8631 and authentication-international is a separate conditional rate. Each business phone number has 1,000 free delivered service messages per month; this allowance resets monthly, does not roll over and does not cover utility/authentication templates. Eligible service replies require an open 24-hour window. Utility templates within that window are also charged under the October change. Charges depend on delivery, not merely an accepted send request. [Current Meta pricing and INR rate cards](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing?locale=en_US), [October non-template/utility changes](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages?locale=en_US).

Maintenance receipts and confirmation of already received money can use approved utility templates; a specific maintenance due reminder needs approved transactional wording. Meta's actual approved template category controls the price, rather than a portal label or non-commercial society status. General notices must use their actual approved category, which may be marketing. The app does not initiate payments. Avoid duplicate receipt and confirmation messages for the same event. OTP delivery and a resident WhatsApp support inbox are separate later workflows, not automatically added by these pricing examples.

These list rates exclude applicable taxes and any intermediary fees. For budgeting only, 118 homes receiving three charged utility messages each is 354 × ₹0.115 = ₹40.71 in Meta delivery charges. Read the current account currency, approved categories, rate card and actual provider agreement at activation; this estimate is not a fixed subscription, bill or guarantee. The general WhatsApp Business marketing page still contains the prior free-service wording, so the dated developer documentation and current rate card take precedence for this plan. Keep the downloaded evidence private and out of Git.

Recheck the exact frozen source, independent publication, current sender/reviewer authority, recipient identity/membership, financial entitlement where relevant, contact revision and channel/purpose permission immediately before a new handoff. ## Provider handoff, callbacks and recovery

Send approved templates using Meta's official messages endpoint with server-side credentials. Validate bounded request/response bodies, timeouts and exact destination/template configuration. Retain portal attempt identity and returned provider message identity without logging secrets, private addresses or message text. Provider acceptance is not delivery. A delivered/read state requires authenticated provider evidence. Map definitive errors to truthful failed outcomes; a timeout, interrupted response or crash after an external acceptance can leave an uncertain outcome and must not trigger blind resending. Do not promise exactly-once external delivery without provider support.

Handle the official verification challenge and verify callback signatures over the original request body using the separately held application secret. Deduplicate provider events, reject invalid signatures/unknown message identities, retain original event metadata and avoid regressing delivered/read state on delayed callbacks. A retry of the same accepted portal operation creates no second approved proposal or dispatch claim. Current consent withdrawal stops new handoffs while already accepted external copies remain accurately recorded. Restore must retain uncertain jobs and require reconciliation before a new external attempt.

Live configuration is disabled by default. Development tests call a local fixture server, never graph.facebook.com. An explicit live deployment decision with real account inputs is required before external delivery; missing inputs do not block adapter implementation, local contracts or the other planned workflows. No paid service subscription is purchased as part of local development.

The current CLI permits only a synthetic loopback preview. Keep that restriction during this provider-preparation checkpoint: exercise the official protocol against a separately configured loopback fixture, with explicit provider-test labels, and reject external origins in that preview mode. The production adapter boundary can be implemented independently, while actual production startup and live activation follow their own acceptance. Default preview behaviour and its existing simulated outcomes remain available.

Freeze the provider identity and exact approved template name, language, category and body alongside each reviewed proposal. Verify actual `APPROVED` status and exact components through the provider's template API; a portal label or a configuration assertion alone is not template approval. Render the resulting template text in the human review. A template body/category/provider change requires fresh review and cannot silently alter an approved message. The current official messages documentation uses Graph v26.0 in its examples; keep the supported version explicitly configured and verify it at live activation. Credentials stay server-side. [Meta template fundamentals/status checks](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/overview?locale=en_US), [template categorization](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-categorization?locale=en_US).

Meta acceptance of a message request is distinct from delivery, which arrives through message-status webhooks. Verify signed callbacks against the original bounded body and the intended account/phone identity. The official webhook documentation permits payloads up to 3 MB and retries for up to seven days: a delayed authenticated retry must not be rejected solely by the simulation's five-minute timestamp rule. Retain deduplication and ordering across restart/restore, with no silent partial acceptance of an oversized or unsupported batch. Do not invent a provider status-query endpoint; implement reconciliation only from documented provider evidence. [Messages API and response meaning](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/send-messages?locale=en_US), [webhook delivery/retry behaviour](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/overview?locale=en_US).

### First provider implementation

Use a separately held private JSON configuration and the Go standard HTTP client. The current preview accepts only a numeric loopback fixture origin, rejects redirects and keeps `--demo` mandatory. The production client boundary permits the exact HTTPS Graph origin; it is not activated by the preview CLI. Missing source-specific template configuration blocks that channel rather than substituting another source's template. Retain the default local synthetic adapter and all existing historical proposals.

Initially support approved templates with one body parameter, the authenticated original's portal URL (`{{1}}`), and optional static text header/footer. Reject other placeholders, interactive components, authentication templates and unrecognised components until their own human review workflow exists. Read the template's actual ID, name, language, category, status and components through the provider API. Freeze the non-secret provider identity, exact components and rendered text in the proposal and its retained event snapshots. Both approval and each new handoff verify the template again; accepted operation replay retains its original result without performing another provider handoff.

Persist a provider attempt before starting the network request. Include the documented `biz_opaque_callback_data` attempt identity in that request: a signed status callback can identify an accepted request even when its HTTP response was lost. A malformed response, transport interruption or server error remains uncertain; a structured definitive rejection may be retried deliberately within the existing three-attempt limit, respecting a bounded provider retry delay. An uncertain attempt with no network-start record can be proved unsent locally; a persisted start with no conclusive response or signed callback remains uncertain. Reconciliation does not query an invented status endpoint or resend. [Meta status callback correlation](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/reference/messages/status?locale=en_US).

The callback boundary verifies the original body with `X-Hub-Signature-256`, verifies configured account/phone identity and the frozen attempt destination, validates the entire bounded status batch, and commits atomically. Preserve canonical original status metadata, duplicate identity and out-of-order evidence without regressing delivery/read state. A direct read report implies delivery but must remain distinguishable from an independently received delivered report. Accept authenticated delayed retries without the simulation's five-minute expiry. Reject unknown identities or unsupported inbound conversations rather than quietly claiming they were processed. [Meta webhook verification and signatures](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/webhooks/getting-started?locale=en_US).
