## Current-code understanding

Current 0.32 `principal` returns a basic recovery identity before reading checkpoint role grants; `fullPrincipal` and protected HTTP routes withhold private access. The recovery screen offers only status and sign-out. The existing MFA methods use the basic principal and real factors, with current freshness and replay/throttling checks. Ordinary bootstrap requires an empty database and cannot appoint a reviewer into a recovered populated host. Offline MFA recovery records supplied custodian attestations and revokes credentials, but does not establish current society authority. Neither an old password/factor nor an old administrator grant can be substituted for that authority.

`recovery_epochs` retains immutable origins, while `recovery_external_jobs` retains the original job identities/states separately from their hold. Epoch IDs are random and restored timestamps can tie or move backward; their lexical/timestamp order cannot establish the latest recovery authority boundary. A new restore must retain an explicit durable current-epoch ordering. Ambiguous legacy recovery history must fail closed rather than select an older epoch. A fresh matching operational restore can establish a known current boundary while retaining its predecessors.

Current receipt counters, off-site identities, message attempts, source snapshots and confirmed originals remain preserved. Additional financial quarantine guards stay in force throughout this checkpoint. REC-01's writer-before-read pattern remains required for transactions that inspect recovery state and then mutate its journal.

## Independent authority and permissions

Use an explicit offline nomination command on a quarantined database. Require a private, strictly parsed, bounded supplied nomination package bound to the actual current epoch/source, a stable operation identity, current verification/evidence references, bounded supplied appointment terms, two distinct custodian attestations and at least two distinct current reviewer identities. Unknown fields, duplicate identities, ambiguous current epoch, wrong source, expired/future or incomplete verification, unsafe files and non-quarantined targets fail without partial authority or credential changes.

This is the host custodian's explicit trusted input boundary. Software records the supplied current verification and its provenance; it cannot prove that the named people actually hold society authority. Fictional integration inputs exercise the mechanism. Society-owned representatives, physical custody and adopted real verification policy remain production acceptance inputs. The server never receives private custody keys or appoints reviewers through a resident or copied-administrator route.

Support independently supplied new reviewer accounts as well as a deliberately verified existing account, without reviving historical grants or resetting unrelated accounts/factors. Initial passwords and handover material stay in explicit private files, outside stdout/logs/Git. An accepted nomination invalidates the affected earlier sessions once; an exact lost-response retry returns its original decision without repeating that invalidation. Changed input, wrong operation identity and conflicting concurrent nomination remain denied. Supplied distinct identity references support separate-person review; an email address alone does not prove two different people.

Current nominees must complete actual MFA and fresh authentication. Add a separate recovery-review capability and narrow protected routes for their MFA and recovery desk. All ordinary registry, finance, household, document, receipt and dispatch capabilities remain withheld. Non-nominated copied accounts retain the current recovery screen. Nomination expiry/revocation or a newer recovery epoch removes recovery-desk access and invalidates held results/forms. A copied earlier nomination cannot grant authority after another restore.

## Review journal and user outcome

Present the approved forest/ivory interface with a changing summary, clear access-review and unfinished-work views, source-aware loading/error/retry states and bounded lists/history. Verified reviewers can inspect only metadata needed for reconciliation; ordinary private downloads and financial actions remain held. Detail views explain what is retained, what independent evidence was supplied and what another reviewer still needs to decide.

Retain actor-bound versioned proposals for supplied current-access decisions and retained external-work decisions. Current-access proposals identify the supplied person/role/household scope, term and verification references; they do not invent current membership or financial entitlement from the checkpoint. External-work proposals identify the preserved original and supplied evidence/outcome, retaining uncertainty where independent evidence is absent. No proposal or approval can manufacture a storage acknowledgement, claim an uncertain message was delivered, mutate an original capture or create a replacement handoff.

A different current freshly verified reviewer approves or declines the exact frozen proposal with an explicit reason/attestation. Preserve proposal, decision, source binding, original actor and paged history. Editing a decided proposal requires a linked successor rather than rewriting its accepted original. Stale/conflicting decisions fail with current-state reload; response loss deliberately retries the same identity and returns the one saved result. Pending or uncertain writes keep their human inputs and prevent dismissal/duplicate actions until their state is resolved. Approval is clearly labelled as preparation for later cutover; new receipt issuance and external actions remain held.

Actual native WebMCP may report bounded current recovery/review status only. It cannot nominate, approve, publish a roster, release recovery, issue money, retrieve originals, configure custody or expose source/job/account identities, passwords, factors, paths or private evidence. Stable native reads preserve human forms; a changed current epoch, reviewer entitlement or proposal version discards stale results.

- Independent supplied nomination references and source/epoch binding are checked before effects. Wrong/malformed/unsafe/duplicate/expired/conflicting input changes no rows or credentials. Exact acknowledged/lost-response retry records one nomination and one credential invalidation. New accounts have no ordinary administrator or finance grant.
- Current epoch ordering survives timestamp ties, rollback, restart and nested snapshot/restore. Ambiguous legacy history cannot select old authority. All predecessor origins and nominations remain preserved.
- Proposer self-approval, stale versions, changed frozen content, wrong operation identity and concurrent conflicting decisions are denied. Separate current review succeeds once; its audit/source/proposal/history remains immutable. A supplied access review does not silently turn an old household/role grant into live authority.
