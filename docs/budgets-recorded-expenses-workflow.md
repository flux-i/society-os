## Budget and original paid-expense records

Cash and other methods remain distinct; every record requires a supplied transaction or cash-voucher reference. A different Treasury reviewer confirms the supplied record. A pending, declined or withdrawn expense contributes no spending. Existing community EXPENSE requests remain estimates/proposals and never count as actual spending.

Concurrent corrections must not both replace the same effective original.

## Meaning of the comparison

Derive confirmed collections from distinct posted RECEIVED entries whose supplied source dates are in the inclusive budget period. Show original received amount, currently reversed amount and current effective collections separately. Opening credit, unpaid charges, pending payment reports, allocations and voluntary contribution attribution do not create additional cash. A receipt attributed across maintenance and a fund counts once. Current reversal state remains current even if its correction was recorded after the source date; label the view as a current operational comparison for a supplied date range, not a reconstructed historical close.

Actual spending consists only of separately confirmed current paid-expense records linked to the budget, with original/correction links retained. Define collection variance as actual minus planned collections and expense headroom as planned minus recorded spending. Display both and collections minus recorded expenses in exact paise, including legitimate negative differences. This difference is not the society's bank balance: it excludes opening cash, transfers and unrecorded activity. Show the date basis, approved plan version and current update time. An unavailable source is unknown and has a local retry; never replace it with zero.

Do not sum independently selected periods or use display pagination for full counts/totals. Changes to a receipt, its reversal or an accepted expense/correction must refresh the comparison consistently. Preserve separate current authority for every list, detail, aggregate and source link.
