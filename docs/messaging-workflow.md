# Targeted notices and receipt sharing — before-code expectations

Recorded before implementation on 6 October 2026. Messaging is accepted locally in release 0.16/schema 15; [executed evidence and limits](messaging-baseline.md) record its checks and retained preview. It fulfils the [operations roadmap](society-operations-roadmap.md) and follows the [contact and messaging contract](contacts-messaging-workflow.md). Preserve the continuing accepted preview and use disposable synthetic data for development dispatches. Real-provider activation remains separate.

## User outcome

An authorised operator chooses an already approved notice or an original received-money receipt, chooses WhatsApp/email and the intended audience, inspects the exact content and recipient preview, and submits a delivery proposal. A different eligible reviewer approves the frozen proposal. A permitted operator can then dispatch it through the configured development simulation and inspect retained delivery outcomes. No money is initiated, charged or received by sharing a receipt. No message leaves the development machine.

The initial external content uses an authenticated portal link. A notice may include its deliberately published title; a receipt message uses a generic private-receipt prompt without payer, flat, amount or evidence. The exact envelope is visible in the human review. Original attachments and statement sharing are separate extensions after their own publication/permission checks. The proposal cannot use an incident allegation, private evidence, draft, archived source or committee-only notice.

## Authority and exact audience

Published notice composition/review requires current community review authority. Receipt composition/review requires current Treasury posting authority; registry/contact administration and ordinary resident receipt access do not grant a mass-sharing decision. Preparation, review and dispatch require current enrolled factors/recent confirmation. Proposer and reviewer must differ. Current authority is checked inside each writer transaction and before accepted-operation replay. Residents can inspect only their own permitted delivery metadata and can stop future messages through their retained contact preferences.

Available targeting: all current people, wing, owners, tenants, selected homes or selected people. Each target is intersected with the approved source's actual audience and current relationship. A person with two homes appears once. Selecting all people for a tenant-only notice does not broaden the notice; an owner-only selection for that source yields no eligible people. A receipt is restricted to its original home and each person's current financial entitlement. A tenant without that entitlement may receive an approved general financial statement later, but cannot receive another home's private receipt.

Because initial links require authentication, recipients need a current active linked portal identity. Missing/suspended accounts are explicit omissions, not invented successful deliveries. The preview separates target people, source-entitled people, contact-eligible people, unique destinations and omissions. Omission reasons include no current/source entitlement, no active portal identity, missing destination, pending/declined/withdrawn verification and channel/purpose opt-out. Finance previews expose only source-entitled identities and necessary destination indicators; they do not create a private contact-directory grant.

Every recipient has independent recorded permission. Shared destinations deduplicate only for the same channel, purpose and frozen authorised content, retaining each person's eligibility decision. Different private sources are not merged. In particular, one joint owner opting out must not erase another person's independently permitted generic portal notification, and it must not be counted as delivery to the person who opted out.

## Frozen review and dispatch boundary

Store the source identity/version, target, channel/purpose, exact envelope, distinct recipient identities, contact revisions, destination grouping, actor identities and review history. Review shows these frozen values, the delivery effect and deliberate attestation. A changed source/recipient snapshot invalidates approval or is omitted according to its recorded dispatch reason; an old decision never silently switches to a newly registered destination. A stale review reload retains the human reason and clears its attestation.

Immediately before each new dispatch claim, recheck source availability/audience, current approved authority, recipient identity/membership/record access, contact revision and permission. Contact revision changes suppress the old destination rather than substitute a new one. Account suspension, opted-out permission, ended financial entitlement and archived sources stop new handoffs. An external handoff has a real boundary: already accepted outside copies cannot be recalled by a later portal opt-out. Retain that outcome accurately instead of promising cross-system atomic revocation.

Operation identities are actor-bound and stable through lost responses. Changed payload under the same identity conflicts. Accepted retries create one proposal/approval/dispatch claim; they do not create another envelope. Unknown client-write outcomes freeze dismissal and edits until the same identity is resolved. Private contact/envelope/review details stay in scoped history; broad audit contains identities, versions and counts, not addresses or private message text.

## Delivery truth and retries

Distinguish awaiting review, approved/queued, claimed, provider accepted, delivered, explicitly reported read, definitively failed, skipped/ineligible, opted out, cancelled and uncertain. Provider acceptance is not confirmed delivery, and no callback means no read receipt. Preserve attempt/event history and provider identifiers. Cancel only envelopes not handed off. Failed definite outcomes permit bounded retries; unknown outcomes require reconciliation before another attempt. A process restart after an unresolved handoff must retain uncertainty rather than blindly resend.

Development uses a provider-neutral synthetic adapter with explicit simulation labels throughout the UI and stored events. Cover accepted, delivered, read, rejected/bounced and timeout-after-acceptance outcomes. Reconciliation of a synthetic accepted handoff changes its known status without a second provider attempt. An unconfigured live channel remains unavailable and never returns simulated success as a real delivery. Society-owned number/domain, credentials, approved templates and activation remain separate production inputs; review current provider documentation at real adapter implementation.

Callback fixtures use a private signing key, bounded payload/time, replay protection and event identity. Invalid signatures or unknown message identities cannot update delivery. Duplicate events are harmless; out-of-order accepted/failure callbacks never regress delivered/read state. Report only timestamps/events actually supplied. Synthetic callbacks remain explicitly synthetic and cannot bypass approval to manufacture a real send.

## Independent acceptance cases

An owner in two homes is one person; two independently eligible people sharing one destination produce one same-content envelope. Owner/tenant/wing/selected-person/selected-home filters intersect the source. A tenant-only notice with an owner-only target produces zero recipients. Private receipt targets never include a non-entitled home/person, even with an otherwise verified finance preference. Default-off contact permissions remain off.

While queued, separately change contact, opt-out, end a membership/finance flag, suspend an account, revoke an operator/reviewer appointment, remove a factor or archive the source. Assert the precise suppression and preserved original envelope/history. Test simultaneous dispatch, response loss after an accepted claim, restart with an unresolved handoff, definitive failure retry bounds and uncertain reconciliation without a duplicate provider attempt. Callback cases cover bad signatures, expiry, unknown/duplicate identity and out-of-order delivery/read/failure.

Exercise actual composition/review/dispatch/reconciliation controls and every relevant opened menu at 1,440px, 768px, 375px and 320px, including short phones. Verify selection, hover, focus/keyboard, exact preview counts, scrolling/dismissal, loading/empty/error/local retry, stale attestation, unknown-write locks and allow/deny/current-scope behavior. Actual native Chrome WebMCP exposes bounded scoped status/count metadata only, omitting destinations, envelopes, private sources and history; it cannot compose, approve, dispatch or reconcile. Test native discovery, execution, cancellation and held-result revocation.

Run backend/HTTP/meaningful recovery, TypeScript/build, full ordinary regression and actual native WebMCP before closure. Capture and inspect final screenshots, fix observed defects and rerun affected checks. Promote a matching binary/assets pair only after consistent pre/post restoration of existing records and new delivery/history/uncertainty state. Record finite coverage and provider/target-host limits; a written contract alone is not acceptance.

## Implementation decisions fixed before the new schema

The first adapter is an explicit local simulation. Live WhatsApp/email remain unconfigured and unavailable. Proposals use the existing authenticated `#community?notice=…` and `#receipts?entry=…` links, scoped source choices and a server-computed preview fingerprint. People without a current active portal account are omitted. Submission and approval recheck the complete fingerprint. A proposer may refresh a pending snapshot; older immutable recipient/envelope rows remain retained and the separate reviewer must attest the new version. Changed destinations are suppressed after approval rather than replaced.

Recipient/history pages are bounded; dispatch claims at most 25 envelopes per decision. Failed definite outcomes permit at most three attempts; unresolved claims become unknown on startup and require reconciliation. Synthetic handoffs persist separately from portal outcome completion so restart/response-loss cases can prove one handoff identity. Signed simulation callbacks use a separate private key, bounded body, five-minute timestamp window and immutable event IDs. Finance recipient previews mask destinations unless the operator separately has contact-review authority. Receipt reversal stops new sharing; original receipts/history remain retained. These decisions do not claim implementation or acceptance.
