# Registered contacts and deliberate messaging — before-code expectations

**Recorded before implementation:** 6 October 2026. Contact registration/consent is now [accepted locally in 0.15/schema 14](contacts-baseline.md). Delivery remains the next workflow; this brief records its original expectations. This implements the user's registered-number, targeted notice and receipt-sharing requirements in the [operations roadmap](society-operations-roadmap.md). Preserve the accepted 0.14 preview while developing against disposable synthetic databases.

## First complete workflow: contact registration and communication choices

A resident can register a phone/email and choose community and financial communications separately for WhatsApp and email. Every choice defaults off. A registry officer can record details supplied off-platform with an explicit identity/permission source. A different current community reviewer must attest the contact identity and recorded permission before the profile is verified. Verification is an attested offline check, not an OTP or a claim of provider verification.

Changing an address or adding permission creates a fresh pending profile revision and suspends delivery eligibility until independent verification. Existing approved values remain in retained history; the changed contact is never silently treated as verified. Decline/withdrawal retains the proposal and decision. Residents and current community reviewers can immediately record an opt-out for a channel, purpose or all communications; stopping messages does not await approval. Restoring permission requires a new proposal and independent review.

Community review authority permits the private contact register and separate verification. Registry authority permits recording another person's supplied details. Treasury/auditor powers alone do not grant a contact directory, and contact permissions add no financial authority. Existing financial roles remain independently enforced. A resident reads only their own contact/history; belonging to the same home does not reveal another person's addresses. A resident whose membership ended may read their own retained profile and stop messages, but cannot create a newly eligible recipient. Staff can retain scoped historical evidence; new registrations/reviews require a current resident relationship.

The register pages current people with actual OWNER/TENANT/wing filters and distinct-person counts, not a directory fetched wholesale into the browser. Profiles/history are bounded. Review screens show the exact proposed addresses, preferences, supplied permission reference, verification effect and a manual attestation before submission. Neither the proposer nor the target resident can verify their own registration. Current authority, MFA factor, target relationship, expected version and actor-bound operation identity are checked inside each writer transaction and before successful replay. Registration and privileged review/changes require recent identity confirmation. A signed-in person can stop their own messages or withdraw their own profile without a new recent-authentication challenge, including after their home relationship ends. Changed details under the same operation identity conflict.

Contacts are private. Native WebMCP exposes only scoped, bounded status/preference metadata; it omits phone/email, permission/identity references and private history. Discovery follows current community/self scope, with cancellation and current-scope checks before returning held results. No tool edits or verifies a contact.

**Independent expectations:** registration remains pending with zero eligible channels; independent verification enables only the deliberately selected combinations; opting out disables the selected combinations immediately without rewriting the verification/received-money history. A subsequent address change disables all delivery until review. Same-home and former-home access to another person's profile fails. Duplicate/lost-response retries create one version/event, and reviewer revocation or lost factor denies replay. No operation changes a login email, registry identity, monetary entry or receipt.

Phone input requires an explicit international number, normalised to a bounded `+` and digits representation without inferring a country. Email input is a plain bounded address, with no display name, header newlines or control characters. Registering a shared address is not proof of each person's consent; each person's permissions remain independent. Identity reviewers must deliberately confirm the supplied destination. Contact/consent events and prior snapshots are immutable and included in consistent local recovery.

## Following complete workflow: exact targeting and synthetic delivery

Use the independently verified contacts for approved notices to all current residents, a wing, owners, tenants, selected homes or selected people. Composition previews the exact eligible people, deduplicated destinations and omissions before a separately reviewed dispatch decision. A recipient with several homes counts once. The selected target must intersect the immutable source audience; a tenant-only source cannot become an owner message because a broader target was chosen.

Private receipt sharing requires its own current finance authority and each recipient's current entitlement to the receipt's home. Financial statement sharing selects an intentionally published approved immutable version. Community contact authority does not grant private receipt/body access. Default to authenticated portal links. Never export an allegation, original private evidence, draft statement, private ledger or committee-only file as a mass message. Attachment delivery remains an explicit separately reviewed extension with audience and revocation consequences.

At dispatch, recheck the initiating authority, source/version/audience, current membership, destination revision and opt-in. A revoked or changed recipient is skipped; a changed contact is not automatically substituted into an old approved delivery. Deduplicate shared destinations only within the same authorised content/purpose while retaining each eligible person's decision. Source removal and account/permission changes prevent new dispatch without pretending already delivered external copies were revoked.

Persist distinct queued, provider-accepted, delivered, explicitly reported read, failed, skipped/ineligible, opted-out and uncertain outcomes. Preserve operation identity, attempt/event history and provider message identifiers. Bound retries; an unknown provider outcome requires reconciliation before a new send. A manual share click is not confirmed delivery, provider acceptance is not delivery and silence is not a read receipt.

Implement a provider-neutral queue with **synthetic adapters only for development**, including failure, timeout-after-acceptance and signed duplicate/out-of-order callback fixtures. Unconfigured real channels show unavailable rather than fake success. No development action may contact a real phone/email. Live credentials, society-owned number/domain, current templates and explicit activation are separate inputs.

Meta's current published policy requires recipient permission, opt-out handling and approved templates for initiated conversations/outside the 24-hour customer-service window. These are provider activation requirements; the first contact checkpoint stores deliberate preferences and attestation, not a promise that a particular live message is approved. Rechecked on 6 October 2026 against the [official WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/), updated 23 September 2026. Refresh provider-specific technical documentation when implementing its real adapter; a failed documentation fetch is not evidence of an API contract.

**Independent delivery expectations:** owner/tenant targeting and source intersection; one destination for a same-purpose shared contact; separate private content never merged; contact/membership/permission changes while queued; source removal; opted-out suppression; unconfigured providers; authentication/signature failure; duplicate/out-of-order callbacks; rejected/bounced email; uncertain outcomes reconciled without a blind duplicate send. Financial sharing does not create a charge or receipt.
